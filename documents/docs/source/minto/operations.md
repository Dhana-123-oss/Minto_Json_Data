# Minto operations

Customers browse products, manage a server-side cart, check out, view orders, and track delivery. Pickers move orders through `Picking`, `Picked`, and `Packed`. Riders accept assignments, verify pickup, move orders to `OutForDelivery`, and complete delivery. Admins can access operational queues and summary data.

Development accounts use password `Minto@123`: `customer@minto.local`, `picker@minto.local`, `rider@minto.local`, and `admin@minto.local`.
